# Truth and Interactive Intake

TOS inspects first and asks second. It must not require the owner to know technical facts that can be determined from repository, runtime, provider, or evidence inspection.

Each project fact records current observed state, intended target state, truth status, confidence, evidence, declaration source, verification time, and invalidation conditions. Absence of evidence does not prove false. Material unknowns and conflicts activate conservative controls and blockers.

Truth status is one of: verified_yes, verified_no, declared_yes, declared_no, inferred_yes, inferred_no, unknown, conflicting, stale, or not_applicable.

Questions concerning intent, ownership, external authorization, legal approval, future customers, pricing, or risk acceptance require authoritative human or external declarations.
